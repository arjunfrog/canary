1. A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 3 m/s² for 6 s. The distance it covers is
(A) 54 m    (B) 108 m
(C) 9 m    (D) 18 m

2. A stone falls freely from a height of 500 m. With g = 10 m/s², it reaches the ground in
(A) 5 s    (B) 100 s
(C) 50 s    (D) 10 s

3. A ball is thrown vertically upward at 40 m/s. With g = 10 m/s², the maximum height it reaches is
(A) 40 m    (B) 160 m
(C) 4 m    (D) 80 m

4. A force of 40 N acts on a body of mass 2 kg initially at rest. Its speed after 4 s is
(A) 20 m/s    (B) 40 m/s
(C) 80 m/s    (D) 320 m/s

5. A body of mass 4 kg moves with a speed of 6 m/s. Its kinetic energy is
(A) 72 J    (B) 24 J
(C) 12 J    (D) 144 J

6. A spring of force constant 2000 N/m is stretched by 10 cm. The potential energy stored in it is
(A) 20 J    (B) 10 J
(C) 100 J    (D) 200 J

7. A wave of frequency 100 Hz travels with a speed of 340 m/s. Its wavelength is
(A) 34000 m    (B) 1.7 m
(C) 3.4 m    (D) 6.8 m

8. Resistors of 4 Ω and 60 Ω are connected in parallel. Their equivalent resistance is
(A) 64 Ω    (B) 3.75 Ω
(C) 7.5 Ω    (D) 32 Ω

9. An electric heater rated 880 W operates at 110 V. The current it draws is
(A) 4 A    (B) 16 A
(C) 8 A    (D) 13.75 A

10. A wire of length 4 m carrying 10 A is placed perpendicular to a magnetic field of 0.2 T. The force on it is
(A) 16 N    (B) 8 N
(C) 4 N    (D) 0.5 N

11. An object is placed 15 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 10 cm. The image distance is
(A) 6 cm    (B) 30 cm
(C) 5 cm    (D) 25 cm

12. Light of photon energy 5 eV falls on a metal of work function 1.5 eV. The maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons is
(A) 8.5 eV    (B) 1.75 eV
(C) 3.5 eV    (D) 6.5 eV

13. A car moving at 40 m/s is brought to rest by a uniform retardation of 4 m/s². Its stopping distance is
(A) 5 m    (B) 200 m
(C) 400 m    (D) 10 m

14. A capacitor of 2 μF is charged to 400 V. The energy stored in it is
(A) 0.8 mJ    (B) 160 mJ
(C) 0.4 mJ    (D) 320 mJ
